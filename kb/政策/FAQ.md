**Q: What is the frame material? / Will it rust?**
A: 6061-T6 aluminum alloy with oxidized surface, connectors are hot-dip galvanized steel, so it's rust-proof.

**Q: Is the fabric waterproof / fireproof?**
A: Yes. Double PVC-coated polyester, waterproof and UV-resistant, flame retardant to DIN4102 B1 (M2).

**Q: Warranty?**
A: 2–3 years. If the roof cover ages, you only need to replace the PVC cover; the aluminum frame lasts many years.

**Q: How do you fix the tent on the ground?**
A: Depends on the ground: expansion bolts on concrete/asphalt, steel anchors on grass/sand, weight plates on marble/tile. What's the ground at your site?

**Q: Is there any pole inside?**
A: No, it's a clear-span structure. 100% of the inside space is usable.

**Q: Payment terms?**
A: T/T or Alibaba Trade Assurance, 30% deposit, 70% balance before delivery.

**Q: Delivery time?**
A: 25–30 working days after deposit.
